Open-air concert template

Work flat. Check it in 3D.

Draw in a clean 2D plan where distances are easy to read. A small isometric preview sits in the corner and updates as you work. Click it to swap the views.

  • Both views come from one plan, so they never drift apart.
  • Rotate the isometric view to check sightlines from every side.
  • Everything is drawn to scale in metres, with a live scale bar.

The pieces an event plan actually needs

Built around how planners work: real event equipment, quick seating, and clear zones.

75+ ready-made items

Chairs, sofas, round and banquet tables, stages, LED screens, speakers, tents, food stalls, toilets, trees. Every item resizes and recolours.

Truss and lighting

Goalpost, T, L and box rigs, with par, moving-head and spot lights on the beam. Switch on light beams to see what they cover.

Seating in seconds

Turn one chair into rows with aisles, a ring, or a fan. Drag an edge to add rows and columns and watch the seat count update.

Zones, roads and labels

Draw named zones for VIP, food court and parking. Lay roads and paths, add flow arrows and text labels.

Plan on the real site

Show a satellite or street map under your layout and plan to scale, with Google Maps, Esri or OpenStreetMap.

Counts for quotes

The summary lists every item and the total number of seats, ready for hire lists and client quotes.

Start from a layout that already works

Each template opens fully editable. Move things, swap things, delete what you don't need.

From blank plot to client-ready plan

Pick a starting point

Choose a template or a blank plot. Set the venue size, or put a map underneath and plan on the real site.

Build the layout

Download an image or PDF for your client. Your projects stay in your dashboard so you can come back and edit.
